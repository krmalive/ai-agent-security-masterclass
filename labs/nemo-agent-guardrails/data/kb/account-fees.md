# Account Fees and Overdrafts

Standard checking accounts carry a $12 monthly maintenance fee, waived with
a minimum daily balance of $1,500 or a monthly direct deposit of $500+.

Overdraft fee is $34 per item, capped at 3 fees per day. Overdraft
protection transfers from a linked savings account are free. Transactions
of $5 or less never trigger an overdraft fee.

Premium accounts have no maintenance fee and no overdraft fees; overdrawn
balances accrue interest at 18% APR instead.
