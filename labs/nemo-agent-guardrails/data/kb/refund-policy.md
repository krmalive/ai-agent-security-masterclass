# NovaBank Refund Policy (Standard Accounts)

Refunds for disputed card transactions on standard accounts are processed
within 7 business days of the dispute being approved. Customers must file a
dispute within 60 days of the transaction date.

Monthly maintenance fees are refundable once per calendar year as a courtesy
credit, at the discretion of the support team.

ATM fees charged by NovaBank machines are refunded automatically if the
withdrawal failed. Third-party ATM fees are not refundable.
