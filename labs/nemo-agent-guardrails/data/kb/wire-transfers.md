# Wire Transfers

Domestic wires: $25 outgoing, free incoming. Cutoff 4:30 PM ET for same-day
processing. Daily limit $50,000 for standard accounts, $250,000 for premium
accounts.

International wires: $45 outgoing, sent via SWIFT, typically settle within
1-3 business days. Exchange rates include a 1% spread on standard accounts
(0.25% on premium).

Wires above the daily limit require a signed authorization form and a
callback verification to the phone number on file.
