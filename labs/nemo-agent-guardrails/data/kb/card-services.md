# Card Services

## Lost or stolen cards

Freeze the card immediately from the mobile app or by calling support. A
replacement card ships within 3 business days (1 business day for premium
accounts). The replacement is free once per year; additional replacements
cost $10.

## Declined transactions

Common causes: daily spending limit reached ($3,000 standard, $10,000
premium), suspected fraud hold, or expired card. Fraud holds are released
after identity verification in the app.

## Card activation

New cards are activated in the mobile app under Cards → Activate, or at any
NovaBank ATM with the card's PIN.
